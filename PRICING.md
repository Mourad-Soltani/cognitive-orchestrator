## Why the Royalty Is a Strategic Lever (and When to Drop It)

- A royalty aligns incentives – the seller stays invested in the acquirer's success.
- It captures upside if the architecture becomes a $50M ARR product (2% = $1M/year).
- It reduces initial sticker shock for VC-backed acquirers with tight cash.

However, corporate development teams strongly prefer flat fees to avoid revenue-sharing audits. **Counter-strategy:** Open with the royalty-inclusive offer, but on first pushback, immediately pivot to the flat alternative (+$400k). This preserves deal velocity.
